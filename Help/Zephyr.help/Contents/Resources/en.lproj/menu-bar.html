Check what Zephyr is doing

Zephyr has no main window that stays open. Its icon in the menu bar is the status readout, and clicking it opens the panel that holds everything else.

Read the panel

Click the Zephyr icon in the menu bar.

The panel says things about this Mac at the top and things about one account further down, and never says the same thing in both places.

  • The top line is what syncing is doing on this Mac as a whole, and only that: Up to date, Syncing, Sending, Sync issues, or Paused.

  • The messages just under it appear only when something is standing in syncing’s way, and say what that is: a network that costs you to use, a Dropbox Zephyr hasn’t been able to reach for a while, or Zephyr switched off in Finder. Each carries a Get help with this. link to the page about it. None of them is a button.

    Notifications and opening at login are left out. Both are choices about how Zephyr behaves rather than things in syncing’s way, and the Zephyr window lists them with the Finder extension.

  • Accounts lists each linked account with what that account is doing and how many files and folders it holds. Click an account to open it in Finder.

  • The line under an account’s name is that account’s alone: … waiting to send counts the changes it still owes Dropbox — edits, renames and deletions as well as new files — and otherwise it says when the account last changed.

  • A warning under an account is either a problem with the account itself or a count of items that couldn’t sync, and carries its own Get help with this. link.

The icon itself carries the same reading as the top line, so you can tell at a glance whether anything is moving without opening the panel.

Note: A network that costs you something holds back only the work nobody asked for. Files you open still download. See Limit how much bandwidth Zephyr uses.

Actions at the foot of the panel

Action

What it does

Manage Accounts…

Opens the Zephyr window, where accounts are linked and unlinked.

Settings…

Opens Zephyr’s settings. ⌘,

Pause Syncing

Stops sending and fetching until you resume. See Pause syncing.

Snooze Notifications

Holds every notification for 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 8 hours.

Quit Zephyr

Quits. Your files stay in Finder, but nothing new syncs until you open Zephyr again. ⌘Q

A row that opens a window closes the panel behind it; a row that changes something in place leaves it open. Press Escape to close the panel.
