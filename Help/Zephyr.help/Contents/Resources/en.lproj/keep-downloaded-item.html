Keep a file or folder on this Mac

Zephyr downloads a file when you open it, and macOS is free to take the download back when your disk fills. Some things shouldn’t wait for either — the folder you work out of on a plane, the library an app expects to find on disk. Zephyr can keep an item downloaded.

Keep an item downloaded

  1. In Finder, Control-click the file or folder.

  2. Choose Keep Downloaded on This Mac.

The item downloads straight away, without anything having to open it. From then on it stays downloaded: a newer version saved on another device arrives here on its own, and macOS leaves the copy alone when it reclaims disk space.

Everything inside a folder you keep comes down with it, including files added to it later from another device. A kept folder therefore takes up as much of your disk as it holds, and goes on growing as the folder does.

Note: The command isn’t offered for an item you have taken out of Dropbox. There is no copy in Dropbox to keep up with, so there is nothing to keep downloaded — see Put an item back on Dropbox.

Stop keeping an item downloaded

  1. In Finder, Control-click the item.

  2. Choose Stop Keeping Downloaded on This Mac.

Nothing is deleted and nothing leaves your Dropbox. The copy on this Mac stays where it is, and the item goes back to behaving like everything else in your Dropbox: macOS can free up the space when it needs to, and you can hand it back yourself with Remove Download — see Open a file, and free up space again.

Keeping an item, and taking one out of Dropbox

Two commands in the same menu read alike and do opposite things.

  • Keep Downloaded on This Mac keeps a Dropbox item here as well. It is still in your Dropbox, still on your other devices, and still synced in both directions.

  • Delete from Dropbox, Keep on This Mac takes the item out of Dropbox and leaves it here alone, synced nowhere.

An item can be one or the other, never both, so choosing either command clears the other.
