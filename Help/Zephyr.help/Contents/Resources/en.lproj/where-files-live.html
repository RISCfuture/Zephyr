Where your Dropbox files are kept

Zephyr doesn’t keep a copy of your Dropbox on your disk. Your files appear in Finder in full, and each one downloads the moment you open it.

Find your Dropbox

  1. Open a Finder window.

  2. In the sidebar, under Locations, click the row reading Zephyr followed by your account’s email address — Zephyr (you@example.com).

    You can also click Zephyr in the menu bar and click the account, which opens the same place.

Everything in your Dropbox is listed, including folders nothing has ever downloaded. Sizes, dates, and folder contents all come from Zephyr’s index of the account rather than from files on your disk.

What’s actually on your Mac

Only what you’ve opened. A file you haven’t opened takes no disk space, and a 40 GB Dropbox can sit in the sidebar of a Mac with 5 GB free.

The files live in your home folder under Library/CloudStorage, which macOS manages. You don’t need to go there — the sidebar is the place to work — and you shouldn’t move that folder or its contents by hand.

Note: Because the files aren’t a plain folder, some tools that scan disks won’t see them, and backup software may skip them. Your files are in Dropbox either way.

Working offline

A file you’ve already opened stays available offline until macOS frees the space. A file you haven’t can’t be opened without a connection, and Finder tells you so. Open the files you’ll need before you go offline.
