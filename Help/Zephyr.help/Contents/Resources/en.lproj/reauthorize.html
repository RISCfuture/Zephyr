If Zephyr can’t connect to your Dropbox account

A red warning on an account is a problem with the account itself, not with any one file, and nothing in that account syncs while it stands.

Link the account again

If the failure is that Dropbox no longer accepts Zephyr’s authorization — you revoked it, or changed your password, or Dropbox expired it — Zephyr offers the fix beside the warning.

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar, then click Manage Accounts…

  2. Click Link Again… beside the warning on the account.

  3. Approve access on Dropbox’s page as you did the first time.

Nothing is re-downloaded and no exclusion is forgotten. Zephyr picks up where it left off.

Note: No Link Again… button means relinking isn’t what will fix it. Hover the warning to read the whole reason.

If Zephyr isn’t hearing from Dropbox

This one means the connection kept failing rather than that anything was refused. Zephyr keeps trying and clears the warning by itself once Dropbox answers again.

  • Check that this Mac is online, and that another site loads.

  • Check whether syncing is paused — the top of the menu-bar panel says so. See Pause syncing.

  • Check Dropbox’s own status page if nothing else on your Mac is having trouble.

A brief outage never raises this warning at all — Zephyr waits it out.

If the account’s settings can’t be read

A row reading Account settings unreadable means Zephyr found the account but couldn’t make sense of its configuration. Link the account again to rewrite it: click Zephyr in the menu bar, click Manage Accounts…, then click Link Again… beside the row.
