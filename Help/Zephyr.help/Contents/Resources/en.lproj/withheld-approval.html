If Zephyr is waiting for your permission

Three things Zephyr needs are granted by you in System Settings rather than by Zephyr. When macOS is withholding one, Zephyr says so under Needs Attention in the menu-bar panel, and along the bottom of its window, where the row carries an Open System Settings button — and says nothing at all when everything is in order.

Grant one

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar.

  2. Click the row under Needs Attention — Enable Zephyr in Finder…, Allow Zephyr’s Notifications…, or Allow Zephyr to Open at Login…

    System Settings opens at the pane that grants it. Zephyr can open the pane; only you can turn the switch on.

  3. Turn on the setting, then return to Zephyr.

Zephyr checks again each time the panel opens and when its window comes forward, so the warning clears on its own once you’ve done it.

Why Zephyr can’t just ask

macOS shows an app’s permission prompt once. After you’ve answered, an app asking again gets the same answer without anything reaching the screen — which is why Zephyr sends you to System Settings instead of prompting a second time.

Zephyr also only mentions what you have actually withheld. Something it has never asked you for is a preference, not a refusal, and it stays quiet about that.
