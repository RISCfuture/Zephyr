If Zephyr can’t reach Dropbox

Zephyr keeps a connection open to hear about changes made elsewhere. When that connection has been failing for several minutes, the menu-bar panel says so. Nothing is wrong with your files and nothing is lost — changes you make now are sent once Dropbox answers again.

What to check

  1. Check that this Mac is online, and that another site loads.

  2. Check whether syncing is paused. See Pause syncing.

  3. Check Dropbox’s own status page, if nothing else on your Mac is having trouble.

Zephyr keeps trying throughout, and clears the message by itself the moment Dropbox answers. There is nothing to press.

Why a brief outage says nothing

A lid that has just opened, or a radio still finding its network, is not trouble — it is the ordinary gap around a sleep, and it ends on its own within seconds. Zephyr waits those out silently and only says something once the wait has gone on long enough to be worth your attention.

Note: A message about reaching Dropbox is about this Mac’s connection, so it appears once at the top of the panel rather than under each account. A warning under one account, while the others are fine, is that account’s own problem — see If Zephyr can’t connect to your Dropbox account.
