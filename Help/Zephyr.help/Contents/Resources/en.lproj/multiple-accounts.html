Use more than one Dropbox account

Zephyr syncs every account you link at the same time. Each one gets its own place in the Finder sidebar and its own index.

Link another account

Linking a second account works exactly like the first — see Link your Dropbox account. You sign in to Dropbox in your browser once per account.

After linking, switch Zephyr on for the new account under File Providers if macOS asks; see Turn on Zephyr in Finder.

Telling them apart

  • In Finder: each account appears in the sidebar as Zephyr followed by that account’s email address, so the address is what tells two of them apart.

  • In the menu bar: the panel lists every account with its own status line and counts. Clicking one opens that account in Finder.

  • In settings: Not Syncing is per-account, and names each account above its own controls when more than one is linked. Bandwidth is not: the transfer limits belong to this Mac, and every account shares them.

The menu-bar icon reads the busiest account, so one account uploading shows as activity even while the others are idle.

Working with the command-line tool

zephyr acts on the only linked account unless you pass --account, so with several linked you’ll want to name one. zephyr auth list shows the identifiers to use, and shell completion offers them.
