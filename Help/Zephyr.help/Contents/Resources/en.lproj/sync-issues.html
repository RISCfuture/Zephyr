If a file won’t sync

When Zephyr can’t sync something it keeps the item and the reason rather than retrying silently. A count appears under the account, and the list behind it says which items and why.

See what couldn’t sync

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar.

  2. Click the couldn’t sync row under the account.

    You can also choose Account ▸ Sync Issues from the menu bar while a Zephyr window is open.

Each row names the item by its path in your Dropbox and says what went wrong underneath.

Deal with an item

  • Show reveals the item in Finder, so you can see what state it’s in.

  • Dismiss takes the row off the list. It doesn’t fix anything — use it once you’ve dealt with the item, or when the reason no longer applies.

Common causes worth checking: the item is larger than your Dropbox has room for, its name uses something Dropbox won’t accept, or the item is a symbolic link, which Dropbox will not take at all. A row saying an item can’t be shared until the account’s email is verified is waiting on you rather than on Zephyr — verify the address with Dropbox and it clears.

A file changed in two places at once is not a failure and doesn’t appear here. Dropbox keeps both and names the second copy itself — see If the same file was changed in two places.

A failure at the top of the list

A red row above the items is a problem with the account itself rather than with any one file, and nothing below it can sync while it stands. Deal with that first — see If Zephyr can’t connect to your Dropbox account.

Start the account’s index over

If the list is long and nothing about it makes sense, rebuilding what Zephyr knows about the account is the blunt instrument.

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar, then click Manage Accounts…

  2. Control-click the account, then choose Rebuild Index…

Zephyr reads the account from Dropbox again from scratch. Your files aren’t touched; only Zephyr’s record of them is replaced.
