Choose between the two versions of Zephyr

Zephyr comes two ways. They sync identically and their interfaces are the same; two things differ, both because of App Store policy.

The App Store doesn’t allow an app to update itself or to install anything onto your PATH, so the App Store version does neither. Wherever a control only the downloadable version can offer would be, the App Store version says so in place of the control.

Get the downloadable version

It is published at riscfuture.github.io/Zephyr, alongside every earlier release.

Install only one

The two are the same app as far as macOS is concerned — same identifier, same Finder registration. With both installed, macOS picks which one serves your files, and it may not pick the one you’re looking at.

Switch from one to the other

  1. Install the new copy.

  2. Open it, and confirm your account still appears in Finder.

  3. Only then, delete the old copy.

Note: Deleting the old copy first can take the Finder location with it, which means linking your account again. The order matters.

You don’t need to unlink anything to switch. Both versions read the same accounts and the same index.
