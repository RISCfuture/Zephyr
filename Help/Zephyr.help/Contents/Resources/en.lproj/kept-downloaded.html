See what’s kept on this Mac

An item you keep downloaded looks like any other item in Finder, so a folder you pinned months ago is holding disk space with nothing to say so. Zephyr keeps the list in one place.

See the list

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar, then click Settings…

  2. Scroll to Kept Downloaded.

Each kept item is listed by its path in your Dropbox. If nothing is kept, the section says so.

Stop keeping an item downloaded

  1. Click Stop Keeping beside the item.

Nothing is deleted and nothing leaves your Dropbox. The copy on this Mac stays where it is, and macOS is free to reclaim the space the next time your disk fills.

What keeping an item does

  • It downloads now, rather than waiting to be opened.

  • It stays downloaded as it changes on Dropbox, so a version saved on another device arrives here without being asked for.

  • It isn’t removed when macOS reclaims disk space.

A kept folder holds everything inside it on this Mac, including whatever is added to it later, so the space it takes grows with the folder. That is the whole commitment: a kept item is otherwise an ordinary Dropbox item, in your Dropbox and on your other devices.
