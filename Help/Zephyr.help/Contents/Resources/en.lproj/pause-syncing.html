Pause syncing

Pausing stops Zephyr uploading and downloading — on a metered connection, say, or while something else needs the bandwidth. Finder keeps serving the files it already holds.

Pause and resume

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar.

  2. Click Pause Syncing.

    The panel stays open, and the top line says syncing is paused.

  3. To start again, click Zephyr in the menu bar and click Resume Syncing.

Pause from Control Center

Zephyr adds a Pause Syncing switch you can keep in Control Center, so pausing doesn’t mean finding the menu bar item first.

  1. Open Control Center and click Edit Controls at the bottom.

  2. Type Zephyr in the search field, then drag Pause Syncing into Control Center or onto the menu bar.

  3. Click Done.

  4. Click the switch to pause, and click it again to resume.

The switch and the menu bar item always agree: pausing in one shows as paused in the other. It works whether or not Zephyr is open.

What a pause does and doesn’t do

  • Files already downloaded stay open-able. A file that hasn’t been downloaded can’t be opened while syncing is paused.

  • Edits you make are not lost. They upload when you resume.

  • Changes made on your other devices are not lost either. Zephyr catches up when you resume.

  • A pause covers every linked account. There is no per-account pause.

If the problem is bandwidth rather than syncing at all, a limit may suit you better than a pause — see Limit how much bandwidth Zephyr uses.
