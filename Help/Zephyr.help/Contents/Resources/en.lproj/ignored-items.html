See what isn’t syncing

An item you took out of Dropbox stays out until you say otherwise, and nothing in Finder says so except a badge you have to go looking for. Zephyr keeps the list in one place.

See the list

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar, then click Settings…

  2. Scroll to Not Syncing.

Each excluded item is listed by its path in your Dropbox. If nothing is excluded, the section says so.

Put an item back

  1. Click Resume beside the item.

Zephyr uploads what’s on this Mac and starts syncing the item again, so it reappears on your other devices.

What’s in the list, and what isn’t

  • Listed: anything you excluded with Delete from Dropbox, Keep on This Mac, and anything the official Dropbox client excluded, which Zephyr reads the same marker for.

  • Not listed: the handful of names Zephyr never syncs at all, such as .DS_Store. Those aren’t exclusions you made, so there is nothing to put back.

Excluded items stay on this Mac only. They are not in your Dropbox, so they are not backed up by it — which is the point, and worth remembering.
