Keep Zephyr running in the background

Zephyr is what watches Dropbox for changes. While it isn’t running, Finder shows whatever it last knew about and doesn’t hear about anything you change on another device.

Open Zephyr at login

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar, then click Settings…

  2. Under General, turn on Launch at login.

Note: macOS can override this. If the toggle turns itself back off, or Zephyr doesn’t open at login despite it, macOS is holding the login item for your approval — see If Zephyr is waiting for your permission.

What happens when Zephyr isn’t running

  • Files you’ve already downloaded still open.

  • Files you haven’t downloaded can’t be fetched.

  • Changes you make are noticed and uploaded the next time Zephyr opens.

  • Changes made on your other devices arrive when Zephyr opens and catches up.

Nothing is lost either way; the difference is how quickly the two sides agree.
