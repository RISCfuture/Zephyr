Find out what Zephyr is built on

Zephyr is open source under the MIT License. The components it is built on carry their own terms, and the ones that require a notice are named here.

Prior art

Zephyr is a reimplementation of Maestral by Sam Schott, the open-source Python Dropbox client — MIT. No Maestral code is used or distributed. What Zephyr carries over is Maestral’s judgment: the set of filenames never synced, the length of history kept, and the shape of its change-feed handling. It is the reason this project was tractable.

Swift packages

Zephyr’s direct dependencies:

  • GRDB.swift by Gwendal Roué — MIT. The SQLite layer under the per-account sync index.

  • Semaphore by Gwendal Roué — MIT.

  • swift-argument-parser — Apache-2.0. Behind the zephyr command-line tool’s arguments.

  • swift-async-algorithms — Apache-2.0.

  • GitHubUpdateChecker — MIT.

And pulled in through those: swift-markdown-ui and NetworkImage by Guillermo Gonzalez — MIT.
