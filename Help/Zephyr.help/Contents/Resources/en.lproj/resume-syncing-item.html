Put an item back on Dropbox

An item you took out of Dropbox can go back. Zephyr uploads what’s on this Mac and starts syncing it again.

From Finder

  1. Control-click the item, which carries the Not on Dropbox — this Mac only badge.

  2. Choose Put Back on Dropbox and Resume Syncing.

From Zephyr’s settings

If you can’t find the item, Zephyr keeps the list.

  1. Click Zephyr in the menu bar, then click Settings…

  2. Scroll to Not Syncing.

  3. Click Resume beside the item.

The item uploads on the next sync and appears on your other devices. Anything inside a folder you put back goes with it.
