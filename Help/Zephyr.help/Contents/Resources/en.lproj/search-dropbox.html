Search your whole Dropbox

Finder’s search field searches everything in your Dropbox, including files that have never been downloaded to this Mac. Zephyr answers from its own index, so searching costs no network and works offline.

From Finder

  1. Open your Zephyr location in the Finder sidebar.

  2. Type in the search field at the top right of the window.

  3. Click the name of your Dropbox in the bar beneath the toolbar, rather than This Mac.

Results are ordered by how well they match: an exact name first, then names that start with what you typed, then names that contain it. Files closer to the top of your Dropbox come first.

From the command line

The zephyr find command searches the same index and ranks results the same way.

Items you have stopped syncing are not in the results. They are on this Mac only, and Zephyr leaves them out so that searching your Dropbox describes your Dropbox.
